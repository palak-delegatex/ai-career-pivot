# LinkedIn Comment Paste-Kit — AICareerPivot (AIC-848)

**Purpose:** the second lane of AIC-848 — "post on LinkedIn as comments from my side using Chrome logged into LinkedIn."

**Why this is a paste-kit, not automation:** `bh-headless` (AIC-851) runs a *fresh, logged-out* headless Chromium profile. It cannot post as you on LinkedIn — that requires *your* authenticated session, and posting to your identity is an outward-facing action that needs your judgment per-comment. So the only viable, ToS-respecting mechanism is: **you, in your own logged-in browser, paste a comment and hit reply.** This kit makes that a 20-seconds-per-thread job.

**Source of truth for all claims:** `marketing/distribution/product-profile.json`. Nothing here claims outcomes we can't back (no "landed a job in X weeks", no user counts). Everything maps to real product facts: free resume-based skill-gap analysis, a $19 one-time roadmap unlock (no subscription), 6-month / 1-year / 2-year plans.

---

## Operating rules (read once — this is what keeps it from reading as spam)

1. **Comment where you'd genuinely comment.** These are *replies to other people's posts*, not your own posts. The value has to stand on its own even if nobody clicks the link.
2. **Lead with the help, mention the tool last — and only when it actually fits.** Roughly 1 in 3 comments should have **no link at all** (pure value builds the account's credibility so the linked ones don't get throttled).
3. **Personalize the first line** to the specific post ("The part about [their point] is exactly right…"). Never paste a comment cold onto a thread it doesn't match.
4. **Volume cap: ~5–8 comments/day, spread out.** Bursts of identical text are what LinkedIn's spam filter flags. Rotate templates; don't reuse the same one twice in a day.
5. **Never use the same template twice on the same person's posts.**
6. **Link format:** plain `https://ai-career-pivot.com/free` (the free tool, not the homepage — value-first landing). Do **not** wrap in a shortener (LinkedIn de-ranks shorteners and they look spammy).
7. **If a thread is emotional (someone just got laid off), drop the link entirely** — use the "no-link" variants. Empathy first; the brand association is the payoff.

## What NOT to say (brand + honesty guardrails)

- ❌ No outcome promises: "get hired in 30 days", "guaranteed job", "X users landed roles."
- ❌ No fake scarcity / urgency.
- ❌ Don't disparage other tools, bootcamps, or coaches by name.
- ❌ Don't claim it's free if you mean the roadmap — the *analysis* is free; the full roadmap is a **$19 one-time** unlock (no subscription). If pricing comes up, say exactly that.
- ❌ Never paste the same template twice in one day or onto the same person twice.
